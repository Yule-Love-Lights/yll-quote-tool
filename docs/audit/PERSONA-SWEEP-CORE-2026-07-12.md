# Persona sweep: core journey (2026-07-12)

Three-persona core-journey audit of the Yule Love Lights quote tool. Every screen was walked through three lenses: a price-insensitive luxury estate homeowner (Millionaire), a commercial multi-property owner (Commercial), and an untrained first-day operator (Operator). Method: drive the live prod app in a browser AND read the source behind every screen, then fan out one finder per journey leg, adversarially verify each finding (real? intentional design? correct severity?), dedupe, and run a completeness critic. Report only. No code changed, no PRs merged, no real customer messages or payments.

Live coverage: all four customer portals (holiday, permanent, event, bistro) walked on prod via public portal links; operator surfaces (dashboard, /admin/quotes, /quote/new builder, /inbox) read live in the logged-in Chrome session (read-only). Legs that could not be exercised live (approve/e-sign/deposit internals, pay-balance with an outstanding balance, per-vertical builder switching, amend/decline/rebook, the WordPress lead form) were code-grounded. The full coverage map is at the end.

## 1. Executive summary

**Severity counts:** 0 Critical, 1 High, 13 Med, 17 Low (31 confirmed findings after dedupe and adversarial verification). 3 candidate findings were dropped in verification (2 false positives, 1 intentional design). 20 of the 31 carry a "needs Naldo" product-decision flag rather than being clear defects.

Note on the two finder-flagged "Criticals": both were downgraded in verification. The event-portal $0 state (PS-C1) is a customer-facing trust defect, not a money loss (the booking and deposit are financially intact from the frozen snapshot). It is ranked High.

### Top 5 to fix now

1. **[High] A staff-approved event, permanent, or bistro portal renders $0, every line item OFF, a blank package heading, and a "select items to continue" gate on a quote the customer already approved and paid a deposit on.** Root cause: `buildApproval` defaults the frozen packageId to `'C'` (a holdover from the holiday A/B/C/D tier ladder) whenever the approval snapshot has no `customerSelection`, and the staff-approve path deliberately omits `customerSelection`. Event/bistro only have package `'D'`; a no-back permanent has no `'C'` either, so the selection seeds empty. This is the exact state observed live on the seeded booked event portal. (`src/lib/portal/adapter.ts:380`, `src/components/portal/SelectionContext.tsx:168-169`, `src/app/api/quotes/[id]/staff-approve/route.ts:116-125`)
2. **[Med] The default holiday portal heading reads "Your Our Recommendation — line by line."** The heading template hard-prepends "Your " to the package name, and package D is literally named "Our Recommendation", so most first-time holiday customers land on a broken-grammar heading on the highest-touch card. The team already solved this exact problem for the event package but did not backport it. (`src/lib/portal/derivePackages.ts:395`, `src/components/portal/dark/WhatsIncluded.tsx:247`)
3. **[Med] /admin/quotes invites a new operator to delete real customer rows.** The header says "Delete test rows here", but the Delete button renders on every row including real leads, and only booked or customer-approved rows are protected server-side. A first-day operator triaging Sent/Viewed/Declined rows can one-click delete a real prospect. (`src/app/admin/quotes/page.tsx:195,367-373`, `src/app/api/quotes/[id]/route.ts:72`)
4. **[Med] The "Amend order" shortcut is a money-workflow dead-end.** From /admin/quotes it opens the pricing builder only; the actual Record-amendment action (which re-syncs the invoice balance, writes the audit trail, and notifies the customer) lives on a different page with no link from the builder. An operator re-prices, sees the new number, and reasonably believes the amendment is recorded when it is not. (`src/components/admin/PipelineActionsMenu.tsx:82-85`, `src/app/admin/jobs/[id]/page.tsx:329-364`)
5. **[Med] Decline then Approve in the same tab dead-ends the customer on a 404.** The client treats every 409 from the approve route as "already approved" and navigates forward, but the server returns 409 for an illegal transition too (a declined or changes-requested quote cannot move to approved). The forward navigation lands on `/approved`, which 404s because there is no approval. (`src/components/portal/snowglobe/StickyBottomBar.tsx:259-272`, `src/app/api/quotes/[id]/approve/route.ts:365-374`)

Honorable mentions among the Med findings: the operator response-time panel prints a raw Supabase UUID as a teammate's name (PS-E1), and the pay-balance page is a bare off-brand page that never shows the customer the amount before sending them to Valor (PS-D5).

### Top 3 missed opportunities

1. **The commercial / multi-property gap.** The tool cannot represent more than one property or a company buyer. One address field, one flat homeowner record, four residential service types, and a pricing engine (`BUSINESS_RULES`) with flat rates, one 50% deposit, one tax rate, and no volume, portfolio, or net-terms concept. Every portal's own About copy claims Yule Love Lights handles "commercial properties" and lists Marriott, Wells Fargo, CVS, Chick-fil-A, and UBS Arena as partners, yet a commercial multi-site prospect dead-ends at the first screen. This is a stated target market with no product surface.
2. **Premium-tier expression for the estate buyer.** No VIP or estate flag, no concierge path, no "recommend the best and handle it for me" express lane, no signature tier. The presentation is identical for a $1,500 job and a $25,000 job, and the millionaire is asked to toggle seven indistinguishable $95 spritzers line by line.
3. **Trust and assurance at the money step.** No terms of service or cancellation language anywhere in the approve and e-sign flow (the only on-page disclaimer covers satellite-image accuracy), no payment-security or data-handling assurance at checkout, an off-brand pay-balance page, and social proof placed below the lead-capture form. A high-value or procurement buyer has nothing to point legal or AP at.

## 3. Reproduction detail for the ranked findings

**PS-C1 (High), the $0 booked portal.** `buildApproval` (`adapter.ts:374-398`) sets `const packageId = (sel?.packageId ?? 'C') as PackageId` at line 380. The staff-approve route (`staff-approve/route.ts:116-125`) writes `customer_approved_at` plus a snapshot that deliberately omits `customerSelection`, so every staff-approved quote hits the `?? 'C'` default. Customer self-approve (`approve/route.ts:279`) always writes `customerSelection`, so it is unaffected. Event derives a single package id 'D' (`event/packages.ts:45`), permanent_bistro likewise (`permanentBistro/packages.ts:33`), and a no-back permanent has no 'C'. `resolveApprovalSelectionSeed` (`adapter.ts:448-457`) passes packageId 'C' with no id list; `computeInitialSelection` (`SelectionContext.tsx:168`) does `packages.find(p => p.id === 'C')`, gets undefined, and returns `selectedItemIds: []`; `activeName` (line 482) returns `pkg?.name ?? ''` (blank heading). Live proof: the seeded booked event portal shows "You're booked, your deposit is in" at the top and, below, $0 subtotal/tax/total/deposit, all four line items OFF, a blank "Your — line by line." heading, and "Our minimum is $1,000. Select items to continue." The booking money is intact (`approval.totalUsd` falls back to `row.total` at `adapter.ts:384`), which is why this is a trust/display defect, not a money loss. A with-back permanent seeds a wrong back-only partial rather than fully empty, because `derivePackagesPermanent` does emit a 'C' back package.

**PS-D1 (Med), the decline-then-approve 404.** `approve/route.ts` returns 409 with code `'already-approved'` (line 345) or `'illegal-transition'` (line 369) when `canTransition(currentStatus, 'approved')` is false. Per `ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS` (`quoteStatus.ts`), neither `declined` nor `changes_requested` can move to `approved`. `StickyBottomBar.tsx:259-272` never reads `body.code`, so both 409s are treated as success: checkout off pushes `/portal/${id}/approved` (which `notFound()`s when there is no approval), checkout on opens DepositCheckout, which at lines 66-70 also treats any 409 as already-paid and pushes the same URL. Reachable because the response modal shows a Close-only confirmation with no refresh, so the interactive bar stays live in the same tab. A page reload self-heals; no money moves.

**Money math, traced clean.** `computeTotalsTail` (`pricingEngine.ts:847-910`): discount is clamped to non-negative and, for a percentage, rounded to cents (`Math.round(subtotal * amount * 100) / 100`, line 863, which also closes the prior "engine rounds percent discount to whole dollars" LOW). Order is subtotal, then manual discount, then early-install promo off the subtotal, clamped to at least 0, then plus rush and takedown to get the taxable amount. Tax is `round(taxable * 0.0875, cents)`, total is `round(taxable + tax, cents)`, deposit is `round(total * 0.5, cents)`, and balance is `round(total - deposit, cents)`. Because total and deposit are both at cents, deposit plus balance equals total exactly. Verified against the live holiday quote: subtotal $1,285, tax $112.44, total $1,397.44, deposit $698.72, balance $698.72, sum $1,397.44. No rounding drift, no order-of-operations error, discount correctly before tax.

## 4. Missed opportunities

### The commercial gap (led)

Concrete, code-grounded absence:

- One address per quote. The builder has a single Property Address (`QuoteBuilder.tsx:2609-2612`) and a flat customer record; a five-property client means five separate quotes.
- No commercial pricing. `BUSINESS_RULES` (`pricingEngine.ts:12-51`) has flat per-foot rates, one 50% deposit, one 8.75% tax, and a seasonal promo. There is no volume, portfolio, or per-site rollup, and the Discount type is only percentage or flat.
- No commercial payment terms. The money split is hardcoded deposit-at-signing and balance-at-install (`QuoteBuilder.tsx:4071-4079`); there is no net-30, PO, or invoice-instead-of-deposit path, which is what a commercial AP department requires.
- No commercial identity in the funnel or dashboard. The lead form and referral form have no company or multi-site field (PS-A2), the dashboard has no account concept (PS-E5), and the quotes list cannot group or roll up sites under one client.

A commercial multi-site prospect can enter the funnel only as a single homeowner and can be served only one address at a time on residential terms. Closing this is an epic, not a fix: an account/organization model owning N properties, a multi-property quote roll-up, a commercial/volume pricing lane, net-terms and PO fields, and a "for your business" entry path.

### Premium-tier expression

No estate or VIP tier, no concierge or "handle it for me" express lane, no way to flag a high-value account, and identical presentation regardless of job value. The luxury buyer is asked to toggle seven identical spritzers (PS-C2) and gets the same snowglobe template as a $1,500 job. Opportunity: a top Signature/Estate tier, a one-tap "recommend the best and handle it" path, and a presentation that scales to job value.

### Trust and assurance

No terms or cancellation language at e-sign (PS-D4), no payment-security or data assurance at checkout, an off-brand pay-balance page that hides the amount (PS-D5), a hardcoded support number in the first email (PS-B3), and social proof below the lead form (PS-A3). ## 8. Method

7 journey-leg finders (Sonnet, one per leg, each reading the code behind its leg and cross-checking the live observations), then an independent adversarial verifier per finding (Opus: reproduce from code, grep for intentional design, correct the severity and file:line), then a plain-code dedupe, then a completeness critic (Opus). 44 agents, 0 errors, 36 raw findings, 33 survived verification, 3 dropped (2 false positives, 1 intentional design). Two surviving pairs were merged in synthesis (the $0 portal root cause and cascade; the "Your Our Recommendation" copy from two legs), giving 31 unique findings. Report only: no code changed, no PRs merged.
