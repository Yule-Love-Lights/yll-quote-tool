---

Team,

We put GPS trackers on the company vehicles. I want you to hear it from me,
with the full picture, before you spot the device under the dash.

**What it is.** A small plug in each vehicle reports where the vehicle is.
The office can see where the vans are during the day, when a vehicle arrives
at a job, and when it leaves.

**Why we did it.** Three reasons, all about running better jobs:

1. When a customer calls asking when we're coming, the office can answer
   without calling you on a ladder.
2. It measures how long jobs really take, so we can schedule days that make
   sense instead of guessing.
3. It backs you up. If a customer ever claims we never showed, we have the
   record that we did.

**What it is not.** The tracker is on the VEHICLE, not on you. It does not
listen, it does not record video, and it is not your time clock.

**Your pay.** You clock in and out the same way you do now, and THAT is your
pay record. Nothing about your pay comes from the GPS. If the GPS and your
clock ever look different, that starts a conversation, not a deduction —
there are plenty of honest reasons they differ, like finishing work inside
after the van is loaded.

**After hours.** The van that goes home at night is tracked around the clock,
because the tracker doesn't have an off switch tied to work hours. I want you
to know that plainly rather than find it out later.

Questions, ask me directly.

— Naldo
